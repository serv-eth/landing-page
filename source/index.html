02 / About us

Support designed for Ethereum.

Serv.eth Support began after founder Micah Zoltu saw user questions going unanswered for hours or days across Ethereum Discord communities, leading to user frustration. Originally Micah tried to help out some of the projects themself by answering questions, but this quickly grew too time consuming. Unfortunately, some applications don't have the volume of questions necessary to hire their own in-house support team, but they also can't utilize traditional support outsourcing services because Ethereum support is too technical.

By creating an Ethereum focused Discord support team, it allows us to be able to immediately provide value to Ethereum applications without lengthy and expensive training on just the basics of Ethereum. Serv.eth closes that gap with specialists who understand Ethereum before they ever enter your server.

You focus on the product. We make sure users feel heard.
